# The lower half of the plot

Below the family house zone, from about 16 m from the road down to P8.

## Slope

Mean 34–44%. The buildable pocket under 50% is roughly **16 × 20 m**, at u 27–37,
v 5.5–19.5 in the local frame. Below u = 28 there are areas over 50% where
nothing may be built.

## The rock scarp

**11.8 m long north–south, face 2.44 to 3.34 m, near vertical at the south
end** — 124% over 2.1 m horizontal. `SH` points mark the top edge at 87.87 to
89.75 m; `BRK` marks the foot.

The main ledge is only about **3 m wide**, too narrow to build on top of.

## The path down

33 m total, 92.9 m down to 84.5 m — **8.4 m of descent**.

| Leg | Gradient | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 27% | steps |
| 2 | 13% | walkable — put the water channel here |
| 3 | 19% | steps |
| **4** | **1%** | **4.5 m flat, directly in front of the rock face** |
| 5 | 57% | steps |
| 6 | 39% | steps |

**Leg 4 is the moment worth protecting.** Level ground in front of a 3 m rock
face is where the walk stops being a route. Any earthworks plan must preserve it.

## The pool

Against the inland face of the scarp, **89 to 92 m** — two to three metres below
the family house courtyard, so the view runs over it to the sunset. The rock
forms its inland edge.
