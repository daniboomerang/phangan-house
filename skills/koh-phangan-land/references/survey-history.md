# Survey history

## June 2025 — do not use

Stated 1,000 m² but its coordinates enclose **957.6 m²**. Corners at 73° and
107°. Every edge an exact round metre, every bearing exact to three decimals.
It was drafted, not measured. Its headline figure — "average slope 46.88%" —
covers a wider area than its own title block.

## September 2025, V2 — current

| Check | Stated | Computed |
|---|---|---|
| Area | 1,000 m² | 1,000.8 m² |
| Perimeter | 130 m | 130.93 m |
| Elevation min / max | — | 80.35 / 96.62 |
| Corner angles | — | 79° to 101° |

71 points, contour interval 0.25 m. Codes: `BR` boundary marker, `TP` topo
point, `BRK` foot of a rock face, `SH` top of one.

Slope computed 38.9% area-weighted over a Delaunay mesh of the 29 points inside
the boundary; the surveyor states 41.10%. The difference is method, not error.

## What is still missing

- **Extension 20–25 m north-east** along the road. Without it no re-negotiated
  boundary can be assessed.
- **Spot levels** at any building corner before committing to a floor level.
  Interpolation between points 3–5 m apart carries about ±0.10 m.
- **Soil and rock depth.** Nothing in the survey covers bearing capacity.
- **Water, power and road connection points.**
