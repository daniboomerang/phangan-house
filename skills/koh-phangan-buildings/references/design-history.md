# What was tried, and why it was abandoned

Saves relitigating.

## The C-house, 14 × 14

Base band 14 × 4 along the road, two arms 4 × 10 running toward the sunset, 6 ×
10 courtyard between. Left arm open to roof at 4.6–4.9 m; right arm carrying a
second floor with the master suite, dressing, bath and a 4 × 4 terrace;
platform over the base band.

Resolved to **three benches — 93.35 / 92.47 / 91.46 m**, steps 0.88 and 1.01 m,
every cut at or under 1.00 m. Courtyard terraced to the room levels, paving 1.2
m against the inner walls only, both far rooms reached by an outside door onto
flat ground.

**Killed by ground coverage.** 196 m² gross minus a 60 m² courtyard is 136 m²
of coverage against a 90 m² cap. 46 m² over.

## Flat floor across the whole footprint

Tested and rejected. A flat floor over 14 m of a 32% slope needs 2.75 m posts at
the low end, putting the roof 8.35 m above the ground beneath it. **Posts spend
the height budget; benches give it back.**

## Raked and stepped roofs

Invented to dodge the height limit. Wrong — the plans specify a flat roof and
5.2 m as the full building height, with the left arm open to it. Abandoned.

## Single-storey 90 m² bar

16 × 5.63 on the contour. Compliant and dull, and it threw away the courtyard,
the platform and the C. **Rejected — it replaced the design instead of fitting
it.** Do not do this again.

## L-shape, 90 m²

Base 14 × 4.4 parallel to the road, arm 4.4 × 6.5 dropping west. Three levels.
Wall 5.21 m, ridge 6.28 m, 2.81 m over the platform. Holds the master plus two
bedrooms at 4 × 5; a third comes out at 18.8 m².

**Live, pending the building-count answer.** If several detached buildings are
permitted, the C returns as two buildings and this becomes unnecessary.
