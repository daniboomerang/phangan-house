# What documents cannot answer

Never present these as settled. Give the user the wording and let the OrBorTor
or their lawyer answer.

## 1. How the 6 m is measured on a slope
Worth about **2 m of ceiling**. See `height-measurement.md`.

## 2. How many detached buildings are permitted on one plot
Worth **90 m² of footprint per extra building**, and possibly the whole site
plan. See `building-types.md`.

## 3. What separation makes two buildings detached rather than semi-detached
No stated distance found. Thai practice treats even an underground beam as
connecting. Ask for a number.

## 4. Whether the land qualifies at all
Zone 3(a) permits building **only** on land privately owned or lawfully
possessed before the 2014 notification. A Sor Kor 1 is a possession document and
filings date from the 1950s, so it should qualify — but this is the gate. If it
fails, nothing else matters.

## 5. Whether a roof reads as Thai, tropical or vernacular
A judgement an official makes. Software cannot check it. Report the
measurable part — pitch present, roof area ≥80% of building area, colour in the
stated range — and flag the rest as discretionary.

## 6. Which text is operative
The 2014 notification as extended, or the 2023 draft if enacted. Percentages
differ between them. Check before quoting an open-space figure.

## 7. Whether an area sits inside a zone at all
Elevation bands are the trigger and are not obvious by eye. The 80 m contour is
the boundary between "no height cap of this kind" and "6 m". A neighbouring
villa that looks the same height may be below the line.
