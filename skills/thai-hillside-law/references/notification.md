# The notification

**Title.** Notification of the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment,
determining protected zones and environmental protection measures in the areas
of Tambon Taling Ngam, Bo Phut, Maret, Mae Nam, Na Mueang, Ang Thong and Lipa
Noi in Ko Samui district, and Tambon Ko Pha-ngan, Ban Tai and Ko Tao in Ko
Pha-ngan district, Surat Thani province.

Those three tambons are the whole of Ko Pha-ngan district. All of the island is
inside it.

**Status.** The 2557 (2014) notification, repeatedly extended, is the operative
text. A replacement draft went to public consultation in May 2023 (ONEP,
`draft-suratthani-20230526.pdf`). The 90 m², the 6 m and the 1 m cut are
consistent across both; verify open-space percentages against the operative one.

## Zones, clause 4

| Zone | Definition |
|---|---|
| 3 (ก) | 80–140 m above MSL, Ko Samui and Ko Pha-ngan |
| 3 (ข) | above 140 m, both islands |
| 3 (ค) | above 80 m on Ko Taen |

## Clause 6(2) — Zone 3

Construction is **prohibited** in Zone 3, except:

- national security and telecommunications
- state utilities
- conservation and forestry works
- **(ง)** on land privately owned or lawfully possessed **before the 2014
  notification took effect**, and then only:
  - a detached house or detached building (**บ้านเดี่ยวหรืออาคารเดี่ยว**)
  - height not exceeding **6 m**
  - ground coverage per building not exceeding **90 m²**
  - open space not less than **50%** of the plot applied for
  - green area not less than 50% of that open space, local trees predominant
  - water retention to reduce discharge
  - **pitched roof** in Thai, humid-tropical or vernacular architecture, roof
    area **≥80% of building area**, colours blending with nature — brick,
    terracotta, brown, grey, leaf green

For Zone 3(ข), above 140 m, the same but open space **≥70%**.

## Clause 6(7) — slope 35% to 50%, Zones 2 to 4

Only a detached house or building, height ≤6 m, and:

- plot **over 400 m²** (100 ตารางวา): coverage ≤90 m², **permeable open space
  ≥70%** of the land, local trees predominant, sustained green ≥50%
- plot **under 400 m²**: coverage ≤70 m², permeable open space ≥50%

**This stacks with 6(2). A plot at 90 m elevation on a 38% slope must satisfy
both — so 70% permeable, not 50%.**

## Clause 6(8) — slope over 50%

Construction of any building is **prohibited**.

## Clause 6(9) — ground level, Zones 2 to 7(ก), slope ≥35%

- ground may be adjusted only where the resulting slope is **not steeper than
  3 horizontal : 1 vertical**
- **prohibited to cut or fill deeper or higher than 1 m from the original ground
  level**, except for building foundations, basements, and underground water
  tanks

"From the original ground level" — ระดับพื้นดินเดิม. Not the finished level.

## Clause 6(10) — trees, slope ≥50%

Damaging the roots or trunks of natural trees with a circumference of 50 cm or
more, measured at 1.30 m height, is prohibited.

## Clauses 13 and 14 — grandfathering

Buildings already existing on or before the day the notification takes effect
are exempt. Permits issued before it continue under the old rules.

**This is why large non-compliant villas exist. It is not evidence the rule is
loose.** A September 2019 task force inspected Samui hillside villas in this
band and found buildings exceeding the height limit.
