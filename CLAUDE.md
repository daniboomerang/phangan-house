# Working in this repo

## Load the skills first

`skills/koh-phangan-land`, `skills/koh-phangan-buildings`,
`skills/thai-hillside-law`. They carry the measured data, the brief, and what
was already tried and rejected.

## Names

**Family house, shala, studio house.** Never "the house" — there are three.

## Rules that keep being rediscovered

- The 6 m limit is tested **at every point**, not once for the building
- **Posts spend the height budget; benches give it back**
- Cross-fall cannot be terraced — keep the width axis near the contour
- Cut and fill max **1.00 m from original ground**, foundations excepted
- Ground coverage caps the **shadow**, not the floor area — stack it
- Land Department coordinates are **Indian 1975**, ~334 m west and 300 m north
- The survey covers **52 × 35 m**. Label anything outside as extrapolation

## Two errors that recur

- **Mirroring.** 2D plans draw local `u` leftward; the 3D must match
- **Ground through a doorway.** The terrain grid must be cut flat under the
  footprint, and fine enough that the cut edge is sharp

## When a constraint bites

Give the number and the trade. **Do not replace the design with a generic one.**
